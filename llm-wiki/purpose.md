# Project Purpose

## Goal

This project is a comprehensive career knowledge base (Career SSoT) covering historical experience. The **Wiki** is the canonical Master Source of Truth. The primary goal is to centralize all historical data to enable the automated generation of highly tailored CV variants (e.g., Engineering Management, CTO, Technical Architecture), cover letters, and provide a structured repository for interview preparation.

## Hierarchy of Truth (Semantic Integrity)

To prevent confusion between roles held and roles applied for, the Wiki enforces a strict hierarchy for fact retrieval:

1.  **Tier 1: Experiences (`wiki/experiences/`)**: The **only** source of factual employment history. If a role is not documented here, it is not part of the professional history.
2.  **Tier 2: Application Artifacts (`wiki/cover-letters/`)**: Archives of specific *applications*. These represent "Targeting" and "Aspirations." They are invaluable for capturing **tone, voice, and narrative style**, but must never be used to verify historical tenure or titles.
3.  **Tier 3: Raw Archive (`raw/`)**: The unrefined source material used for extraction and verification.

### Entity Integrity Rules
- **Linked Creation Only**: Do not create a new `entity` node unless it is linked to a verified Tier 1 Experience. Do not create entities for target companies in cover letters or job descriptions.
- **Consult Mappings**: Before creating any entity, consult **`mappings.md`** to resolve known typos and aliases.
- **Check timeline**: If timeline overlaps after mapping, but the role has a slightly different name, do not create a different experience. Overlapping roles on identical timelines should be merged or flag a conflict query.

### Language Standard
- **English Only**: All structured entries in the `wiki/` directory (experiences, entities, skills, notes, etc.) must be written in English. This ensures consistency and compatibility with the downstream agentic pipeline. If source material is in another language, it must be translated during the ingestion or reconciliation phase.

## Data Flow

1. **Ingest (Inputs):** 
    - **Primary Records (`raw/sources/`):** Raw historical CVs, certificates, and patents.
    - **Supplemental Records (`raw/supplemental/`):** Candidate profiles (recruiter-authored), performance reviews, 360-degree feedback, and Whitepapers.
    - **Aspiration Records (`raw/cover-letters/`):** Historical cover letters used for voice/tone reference.
2. **Structuring (Database):** Information is extracted and reconciled into the `wiki/` structure. 
    - Facts move to `wiki/experiences/` and `wiki/patents/`.
    - Authentic self-reflections and technical methodologies move to `wiki/notes/` (Self-Perspective).
    - Recruiter/Market summaries move to `wiki/notes/` (Third-Party Perspective).
3. **Generation (Outputs):** Tailored CVs, cover letters, and career summaries are generated by the **Agentic Application** (which consumes the Wiki data + a current **Job Description** as external inputs).

## Domain Specialization Tracks

To generate highly targeted output documents (such as resumes, bios, or cover letters), the Career Operating System classifies experiences and skills into discrete industry specializations or tracks. These tracks are not hardcoded, but are rather chosen and defined by the user to represent the main pillars of their professional identity. 

### Customizing Your Tracks
A candidate with a tech-focused career might organize their experiences around tracks like:
- **Management:** Team leadership, budgeting, strategic delivery, and scaling organizations.
- **Architecture:** Systems design, integration standards, platform scaling, and infrastructure strategies.
- **Engineering:** Hands-on software development, performance optimizations, and technical execution.
- **Entrepreneurial:** Prototyping, startup technology strategy, lean MVP building, and founder dynamics.

For other professionals, these tracks could look completely different:
- **Finance:** Investment Banking, Corporate Finance, Risk Management, or Audit pillars.
- **Healthcare:** Clinical Practice, Healthcare Administration, Medical Research, or Public Health pillars.
- **Sales & Marketing:** B2B Enterprise Sales, Product Marketing, Brand Strategy, or Growth Operations.

By defining and tagging experiences with relevant tracks, the downstream agentic generators are able to pull specific, contextually relevant achievements to draft tailored CV variants representing different career paths.

## Scope

**In scope for the LLM Wiki:**
- Ingesting and reconciling historical resumes, patents, and documents into a structured repository.
- Standardizing and categorizing career experience into user-defined "Domain Specialization Tracks".
- Mapping skills directly to specific achievements for evidence-based, graph-relational querying.
- Archiving historical cover letters and extracting authentic personal reflections (`Narrative & Reflections` snippets) to bootstrap experiences.
- Maintaining strict schema compliance across all nodes to provide a predictable dataset.

**Out of scope for the LLM Wiki (Handled by the downstream Python Agentic application):**
- Automated retrieval, ranking, and final synthesis of CV variants.
- End-to-end generation of tailored cover letters for specific job applications.
- Rendering CV variants into PDF or other distribution formats.
- Live job application tracking (CRM functionality).
