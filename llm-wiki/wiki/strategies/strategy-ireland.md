# Ireland Strategy

## CV Format Directives

### Structure & Length
- 2-3 pages maximum.
- Section order: Personal Profile → Experience → Education → Skills & Languages.

### Experience Section
- Use Month YYYY format (e.g., "Apr 2024 – Present").
- Use STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for bullet points.
