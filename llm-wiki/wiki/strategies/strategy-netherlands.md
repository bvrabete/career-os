# Netherlands Strategy

## Professional Tone: "Directness Above All"
- **Style**: Straightforward, factual, and achievement-focused.
- **Avoid**: Vague adjectives and flowery descriptions.

## CV Format Directives

### Structure & Length
- STRICTLY 3 pages maximum.
- Section order: Professional Summary → Work Experience → Education → Key Skills → Languages.
