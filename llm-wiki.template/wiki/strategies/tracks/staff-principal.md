# Staff & Principal Engineer Track

## Positioning & Archetype
- **Target Role**: Staff / Principal Software Engineer, Solutions / Systems Architect.
- **Narrative Focus**: Emphasize technical leverage, high-scale distributed systems, architectural decision records (ADRs), non-functional requirements (resilience, latency, cost optimization), and organization-wide mentorship.

## CV Directives
- **STAR Framing**: Show how technical decisions solved fundamental business constraints or unlocked massive engineering velocity.
- **Scope of Impact**: Detail cross-functional impact (e.g. multi-team adoption, platform standardization, reducing enterprise tech debt).
- **Depth**: Showcase mastery in complex distributed algorithms, microservices orchestration, and resilient cloud architectures.
