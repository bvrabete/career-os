# Executive Leadership Track

## Positioning & Archetype
- **Target Role**: VP of Engineering, Chief Technology Officer (CTO), Head of Technology.
- **Narrative Focus**: Emphasize executive business acumen, enterprise technology modernization, multi-million P&L/budget management, board reporting, M&A integration, and strategic organizational design.

- **Strategic Impact**: Translate technical initiatives into direct top-line revenue growth, gross margin improvements, and market competitiveness.
- **Executive Gravitas**: Highlight visionary technology strategy, talent organization scaling across global sites, and enterprise risk management.
