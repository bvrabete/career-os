# Engineering Management Track

## Positioning & Archetype
- **Target Role**: Engineering Manager, Senior Engineering Manager, Director of Engineering.
- **Narrative Focus**: Emphasize building high-performing engineering teams, hiring and career coaching, sprint delivery cadence, engineering health/metrics (DORA, cycle time), and stakeholder management.

## CV Directives
- **People & Team Scope**: Quantify team sizes, direct reports, hiring velocity, retention, and promotional trajectories.
- **Operational Delivery**: Highlight roadmap execution, cross-functional collaboration with Product/Design, and fostering a blameless, high-trust engineering culture.
