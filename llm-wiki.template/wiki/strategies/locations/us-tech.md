# US Tech Strategy

## Professional Tone
- **Tone**: High-impact, punchy, and metric-dominated.
- **Directives**: Lead every bullet with a strong action verb, precise quantitative outcomes ($ revenue, % latency, # users), and zero filler words.

## CV Format Directives

### Structure & Length
- STRICTLY 1 page maximum.
- Section order: Professional Summary → Key Skills → Work Experience → Education.

### Experience Section
- Keep bullet points to 1-2 lines maximum.
- Highlight scale, speed, and business ROI.
